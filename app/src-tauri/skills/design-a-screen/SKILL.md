# Design a screen

Say what you would change and why before you change it.

1. **Who and what for.** One sentence: who is on this screen, and the one thing they came to do. If you cannot say it, ask.
2. **Read what exists.** Colours, type, spacing, buttons and words the project already uses. Follow any design notes it has. New screens should look as if they were always there; a new pattern needs a reason.
3. **Decide what comes first.** Rank everything on the screen. One main action. What is rarely needed goes behind a click. Remove what serves nobody.
4. **Sketch two or three layouts** in words or simple text boxes, with what each is good and bad at. Say which you would pick.
5. **Open `references/states.md`** and go through every state on it. Most bad screens are only designed for the case where everything goes well.
6. **Write the words.** Real text, never placeholders; see the skill `ui-words`.
7. **Check it small and big:** narrow window, wide window, long names, 1,000 rows, large text.

Hand over a description someone can build without guessing: layout, sizes taken from what the project has, states, words, and what you did not decide. For a ticket to build it, name Jiro the Frontend.
