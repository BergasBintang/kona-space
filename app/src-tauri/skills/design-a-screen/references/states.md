# States every screen needs

Go down the list. For each one, say what the person sees and what they can do next. If it cannot happen, say why.

- **First time:** nothing here yet. Say what this place is for and give the first step. Never just "No data".
- **Loading:** what shows while waiting. Keep the layout still so that it does not jump.
- **Normal:** a typical amount of content.
- **A lot:** 1,000 rows, very long names, words that do not fit.
- **Very little:** one item; one letter.
- **Nothing found:** after a search or a filter. Say what was searched and how to widen it.
- **Failed:** what went wrong, in plain words, whether their work is safe, and what to do next. Offer a retry when it makes sense.
- **Partly failed:** some of it loaded, some did not.
- **Offline or slow:** what still works.
- **No permission:** why, and who can change it.
- **While saving:** the button shows it is busy and cannot be pressed twice.
- **After saving:** how the person knows it worked.
- **Unsaved changes:** leaving the screen does not silently lose them.
- **Dangerous action:** says what will be lost, and has a way back when possible.
- **Hover, focus, pressed, disabled:** for everything that can be clicked. Focus must be visible.
