# Help article

People read a help page when they are stuck, in a hurry, and have one question.

1. **Start from real questions.** Use the words people actually wrote, because those are the words they will search for.
2. **Check it for real.** Follow the steps in the product. Do not write from memory or from the plan. If you cannot try it, say so.
3. **Open `templates/article.md`** and fill it in.
   - One question per page.
   - The title is the question or the task: "Change your password".
   - Give the answer or the first step at once. No history of the feature.
   - Numbered steps, one action each. Use the exact words on the screen, in bold.
   - Say what they should see afterwards.
4. **Cover what goes wrong:** the two or three most common ways it fails, each with what to do.
5. **Say when it does not apply:** only on some plans, some versions, some systems.
6. **End with where to go next** and how to reach a person.

Plain words: see the skill `plain-words`. Date it and note which version it describes, because a help page that is out of date is worse than none.
