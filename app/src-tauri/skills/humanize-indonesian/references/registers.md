# Registers: who is talking to whom, and how

Choose the register **before** you edit. Most "it sounds like a machine" in Indonesian is the wrong register (a stiff form letter in a chat, or forced slang in a bank notice), not a stock phrase.

What was read for this guide (2026-11-23): live pages of Jenius, Gojek, GoPay, OVO, BCA, Telkomsel and Traveloka; Indonesian writing guides and blogs (Purwadhika, Tempo Institute, Digima, Qiscus, Kirimi, Ivan Lanin on Kumparan, Badan Bahasa on mixed language); a public repo of Indonesian writing skills (alvinindra/bahasa-skills, which cites the Gojek, Tokopedia and Traveloka UX posts that could not be opened directly). No company publishes a full style guide, so the company notes below are what their pages **show**, not what they have written down. The do and don't pairs at the end are written for this guide, from those sources.

## The levels

Indonesian linguistics teaches five levels (after Martin Joos): *beku* (law, prayers), *resmi* (official letters, speeches), *konsultatif* (meetings, business, news, blogs), *santai* (friends, social media), *akrab* (family, often unfinished sentences). In practice you write four things:

| Register | Used for | Address | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Resmi** | Official letters, contracts, regulations, academic papers, finance and legal notices | *Anda* or *Bapak/Ibu/Saudara*; or no pronoun | Standard spelling and grammar, no slang, no emoji, full sentences |
| **Konsultatif** | Business email, news, articles, product pages, help centers, reports | *Anda* for finance, B2B, health, property; *kamu* for consumer apps and under-35 readers | Standard grammar, plain words, short sentences, no slang |
| **Santai** | Chat support, social posts, app microcopy for consumers, newsletters | *kamu* (-mu), or *Kak* in chat; *aku/kami* for the writer | Standard spelling with casual words (*aja*, *gak*, *yuk*), tidy punctuation |
| **Gaul** | Only if a brand or a person asks for it | *aku/kamu* or *gue/lo*, never mixed | Code-mixing and slang, in small amounts |

"Baku tidak harus kaku": standard Indonesian can be warm. A text that is correct and stiff is still a machine text.

## Rules that hold in every register

1. **One address form per text** (and per product). *kamu* and *Anda* in the same screen, or *kamu* then *-mu* then *Anda*, is the commonest fault. (Telkomsel's pages mix both; read that as an inconsistency, not a model.)
2. **Capitals:** *Anda* is always capitalised. *kamu* and *-mu* are not.
3. **Emoji:** none in resmi; at most one or two in santai; fewer in finance and health.
4. **Do not type badly on purpose.** Brands that write *gak* and *aja* (Jenius, GoPay) still keep correct capitals and punctuation. Never add typos, *yg*, *mksd*, doubled commas or random capitals to look human. That is *alay*, and it reads as spam.
5. **Do not add chat endings to a document** ("Semoga bermanfaat!") or document endings to a chat ("Hormat kami").
6. **Numbers:** spelled out for anything a reader will hear; *Rp25.000* in a chat or a page; *pukul 14.00*. See `references/official-rules.md`.
7. **Loanword or Indonesian term:** *unduh/unggah/pindai* or *download/upload/scan*, but the same one everywhere in the product. In a quote or a product name, keep it as it is.

## Address and greetings

- **Consumer app or shop:** *kamu*. Finance, insurance, B2B, health, property, LinkedIn: *Anda*. (BCA and OVO use *Anda*; Jenius, Gojek and GoPay use *kamu* or *-mu*; Traveloka uses no pronoun at all, only commands and noun phrases.)
- ***Kak*** is the neutral polite word for a stranger in online selling and chat support ("Halo Kak Sarah"). It suits young urban buyers and can feel odd to older regional customers. It is a chat word, not a product-screen word. For adults you do not know, or for a business customer, *Bapak/Ibu* is safer.
- **Opening a message:** a time greeting (*Selamat pagi/siang/sore*) is safe for a mixed audience. *Assalamualaikum* fits a Muslim recipient or a religious organisation: never put it in a template for a general brand, and answer *wa'alaikumsalam* if the customer opens with it. *Dengan hormat* is the neutral letter opener, and feels wrong on WhatsApp.
- **Closing:** *Terima kasih* is enough. *Terima kasih atas kesabarannya* and *mohon* are normal polite phrases, but they turn into a template when repeated: use them once, with a concrete time or next step ("Kami cek dulu dan kabari sebelum jam 3 sore").
- **Writing to a lecturer or a superior:** greeting, *Mohon maaf mengganggu waktu Bapak/Ibu*, who you are, the point, thanks.

## Chat punctuation (santai only)

In chat, the casual norm is no full stop on a short reply. A closing full stop on "Iya." reads as cold, "..." as hesitation or disappointment, "!!!" as shouting. A support agent's reply is not a casual chat, so write it in full sentences with light punctuation, one emoji at most. Do not copy chat habits into articles, emails or documents.

## Gaul and code-mixing

- Badan Bahasa's advice is one language at a time and standard Indonesian in formal settings. The language commentator Ivan Lanin accepts mixed "Jaksel" talk only inside the speaker's own circle: outside it, the message arrives badly.
- Real South Jakarta speakers do not talk like the online joke. Forced English fillers (*which is*, *literally*, *basically*, *in terms of*) read as a brand trying too hard (*sok gaul*), and slang goes out of date in weeks.
- Write gaul **only when the brand or person asked for it**, in small amounts (one or two touches a paragraph), never in finance, health or formal mail. Choose *aku/kamu* or *gue/lo* and keep to it; do not mix them. *Bro*, *cuy* and *anjay* in every sentence is a parody.
- If you are not sure, write santai, not gaul.

## Natural sentences, not translated ones

- Short sentences, then one longer one that carries a reason.
- Start with the thing the reader cares about, not with a warm-up.
- *punya* and *ada* where *memiliki* sounds heavy; *bikin* only in santai.
- Particles (*sih*, *dong*, *kok*, *deh*, *ya*) in santai only, a few at a time. In konsultatif and resmi, none.
- Real example of a calque and its repair (from a copywriting article): "Identifikasi pain point target market kamu" → "Kenali masalah utama target pasarmu". "Berorientasi sama hasil" is a translation of *results-oriented*; say what the person does.
- A claim that cannot be checked is not copy: "terbaik di kelasnya", "solusi digital terbaik" mean nothing without a proof. Put the proof or a measurable detail in its place, and never invent one.

## Do and don't, by kind of text

These pairs were written for this guide. They show the register, not set phrases to copy.

**App microcopy** (*kamu* for consumer apps, *Anda* for finance; one only; buttons start with a verb and have three words or fewer)

| Tulis | Jangan |
|---|---|
| Kata sandi salah. Coba lagi atau atur ulang lewat "Lupa kata sandi". | Terjadi kesalahan. Silahkan coba lagi. |
| Hapus 3 foto? Foto yang dihapus tidak bisa dikembalikan. [Batal] [Hapus] | Apakah Anda yakin? [Ya] [Tidak] |
| Pesanan dikirim. Perkiraan tiba Kamis. | Selamat! Pesanan Anda telah berhasil dikirimkan! |
| (satu pilihan di semua layar: *Unggah*) | *Unggah* di satu layar dan *Upload* di layar lain |

An error says what happened and what to do. A destructive confirmation names the thing. A joke in an error message can say "we do not understand your frustration".

**Customer-service reply (WhatsApp)**

| Tulis | Jangan |
|---|---|
| Halo Kak Sarah, maaf ya pesanannya telat. Kami cek ke kurir dan kabari sebelum jam 3 sore. | Kami memohon maaf atas ketidaknyamanan yang Anda alami. Mohon kesabarannya. |
| Selamat siang, Bapak. Stoknya masih ada, bisa kami proses hari ini. | Yang Terhormat Bapak/Ibu Pelanggan, dengan ini kami informasikan… |
| Aku belum tahu pasti, aku tanyakan dulu ke tim ya. | (mengarang jawaban, atau memakai templat yang sama di tiap balasan) |

When you do not know, say so. A template that is the same in every reply is the thing customers notice.

**Marketing copy** (one address, one action, one real benefit, no empty superlative)

| Tulis | Jangan |
|---|---|
| Jadwal postingmu sudah rapi belum? Coba gratis dulu. | Tingkatkan bisnis Anda, yuk daftar, kamu bisa mulai gratis. |
| Buka 20 tab tanpa laptop melambat. | Solusi digital terbaik untuk kebutuhan Anda. |
| Sisa 12 pcs di gudang. (hanya jika benar) | Buruan sebelum kehabisan!!! |

**Blog or article** (konsultatif to santai; start with a fact; vary the rhythm)

| Tulis | Jangan |
|---|---|
| Tiga dari lima toko kecil kehabisan stok saat promo. Ini penyebabnya. (hanya dengan angka yang nyata) | Di era digital yang serba cepat ini, manajemen stok menjadi sangat krusial. |
| Alat ini bikin menulis jauh lebih cepat. | Penggunaan teknologi ini memberikan dampak yang signifikan. |
| (berhenti saat isinya selesai, atau tutup dengan langkah berikutnya) | Semoga bermanfaat! |

**Formal document** (*Anda* or *Bapak/Ibu*; no slang; no emoji; correct spelling)

| Tulis | Jangan |
|---|---|
| Dengan hormat, bersama surat ini kami mengajukan permohonan… | Halo Pak, mau tanya soal permohonan ya. |
| Silakan hubungi kami paling lambat 5 Desember. | Silahkan segera hubungi kami ASAP. |
| Kami mengubah jadwal rapat menjadi Rabu pukul 10.00. | Kami melakukan perubahan terhadap jadwal pelaksanaan rapat tersebut. |
