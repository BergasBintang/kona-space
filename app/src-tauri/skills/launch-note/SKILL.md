# Launch note

Nobody cares that you shipped. They care what changes for them. Never invent numbers, customers or quotes.

1. **Pick the reader.** Existing users or strangers? They need different notes.
2. **Start with what changes for them:** "You can now pay by bank transfer", not "We are excited to announce".
3. **Say it in order:**
   - what is new, in one sentence;
   - who it helps, and the problem it removes;
   - how it works, in a line or two, with a picture if one exists;
   - one clear next step, with its link.
4. **Be honest about limits:** what it does not do yet, and any change that could surprise them (a price, a removed feature, an action they must take). Bad news goes up front.
5. **Fit the place.** Open `references/formats.md` and follow the format for where this goes. A social post is not an email with fewer words.
6. **Cut anything they would not miss.** Thanks and the story behind it can go at the end, if at all.

Give the note in the format asked for, and a shorter version too. List any claim that needs checking. Sending or posting is the owner's job, unless they say otherwise.
