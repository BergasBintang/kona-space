# Formats

**Email to existing users**
- Subject says what changed: "You can now pay by bank transfer". No "Big news!"
- First line repeats the point. Under 150 words. One link.
- If they must do something, say so at the top, with the date.

**Short social post**
- The first line must stand alone; it is all most people see.
- One idea. A picture or a short clip if there is one. The link at the end.
- Do not use hashtags as decoration.

**Longer post or blog**
- Title says the benefit. Then the same order as the note, with room for how it works and why it was built this way.
- Include a real example.

**Message in a community or chat**
- Two or three lines, like a person talking. Ask for what you want to hear back.
- Follow the rules of the place; do not post the same text everywhere.

**Change notes in the product**
- Group as New, Improved and Fixed.
- Say what changes for the person, not the ticket title.

**To the press or partners**
- What, who, when, one true number, and who to contact.
