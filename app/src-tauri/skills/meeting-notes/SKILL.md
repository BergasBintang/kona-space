# Meeting notes

People read notes to learn what was decided and what they have to do, not to hear the talk again.

1. **Use only what was said.** Never fill in a decision, a name or a date that nobody said. Mark anything unclear with (not clear), and ask.
2. **Open `templates/notes.md` and fill it in,** most important first:
   - **Decided:** each decision in one line, with the reason if it was given.
   - **To do:** what, who, by when. One person per item. "Someone should" is not a task: flag it.
   - **Still open:** questions with nobody's answer yet, and who could find out.
   - **Worth keeping:** only what someone will need later.
3. **Keep it short.** Leave out who said what, unless it matters.
4. **Say what you will do next.** Offer to turn the to-do list into tickets with the skill `write-a-ticket`. Say what will be written down, and for whom.

If people disagreed and nothing was settled, say that plainly; do not write it up as a decision. Keep passwords and keys out, and be careful with what was said in confidence.
