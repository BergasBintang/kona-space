# Prioritise work

The output is an order and the reasons for it, so the owner can disagree with a reason instead of with the whole list.

1. **List everything** from the tickets and what the owner said. Merge duplicates. Say what you left out.
2. **Ask what matters most right now** if it is not written down: money, keeping users, shipping a date, reducing risk. One goal at a time.
3. **Score each item** on three things, each small, medium or large:
   - **Gain:** how much it moves that goal, for how many people.
   - **Effort:** how long and how much it disturbs. Ask the person who would do it when you can.
   - **Sureness:** how much of this is known and how much is a guess.
4. **Rank:** large gain and small effort first. Low sureness means a small test before the full job.
5. **Look for what the scores miss:** something that blocks other work, a fixed date, a safety or security problem (these go first whatever the score), a promise already made.
6. **Say what will not happen** because of this order. That is the actual decision.

Give a table with the item, gain, effort, sureness and one line of reason, then your top three in words. It is the owner's call: never move tickets as if the decision were already made.
