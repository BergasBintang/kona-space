# The people, and what to send them

| If it is about | Send to | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A vague wish, what to build, what comes first | Kiki the Product | "Should we add teams?" |
| How something looks, its layout, its words | Nokka the Design | "This page feels cramped" |
| Building or fixing a screen | Jiro the Frontend | "The Save button does nothing" |
| Data, server, slow queries, connections | Kit the Backend | "Add an export of all tickets" |
| Trying to break it, a test plan | Sen the QA | "Is this version safe to ship?" |
| Attacks, leaks, keys, risky packages | Bejo the Security | "Is our login safe?" |
| Shipping, servers, something down | Sora the DevOps | "Put this on the server" |
| What to say, launches, a landing page | Yuzu the Marketing | "Announce the new version" |
| Articles, being found on search | Nobo the Content and SEO | "Why are we not on Google?" |
| A user's question or complaint | Kenn the Support | "Reply to this customer" |
| Notes, status, who does what | Kupa the Secretary (you) | "What happened this week?" |

**The usual order for a new feature**
Kiki shapes it, then Nokka designs it, Kit builds the data, Jiro builds the screen, Sen tries to break it, Bejo checks it if it touches logins, money or personal data, Sora ships it, Yuzu and Kenn tell people.

**Small jobs skip steps.** A typo goes straight to Jiro. Do not run everything through everybody.
