# SEO brief

Write for the person searching first. A page that truly answers the question has the best chance. Never invent search numbers: if you have no data, say so and mark guesses.

1. **What does the business want from this page?** A sign-up, a sale, an answer that saves support time?
2. **Who is searching, and what for?** List the words they would type, in their language. Use real sources: support questions, search data if the owner has it, what comes up on a search if you can look.
3. **What do they want?** To learn, to compare, to buy or to find one place? Only one per page. The kind of page has to fit: nobody searching for a how-to wants a sales page.
4. **Look at what ranks today,** if you can search. What do those pages cover? What do they miss? What could we say from real experience that they cannot?
5. **Check we do not already have it.** Improve an existing page rather than compete with yourself.
6. **Open `templates/brief.md`** and fill it in.

Do not promise rankings. Search engines decide, and results take time. Next: `write-an-article`, then `page-seo-check`.
