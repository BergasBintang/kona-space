# Shape an idea

Do not plan or build yet. A vague idea gets built wrong, however good the code is.

1. **Read first.** Look at the project, its tickets and its notes, so you do not ask what is already written down.
2. **Say it back.** One or two sentences: what you think the owner wants and for whom. Ask whether that is right.
3. **Ask one question at a time.** Offer choices when you can, with the one you would pick first. Stop asking as soon as you could describe the result to a stranger. Five questions is a lot.
4. **Find the real goal.** If the owner names a solution ("add a button"), ask what they are trying to get done. The button may still be right; now you know why.
5. **Offer two or three ways.** Each with what it costs and what it leaves out. Say which you would choose and why.
6. **Cut.** Ask what can wait. Name what the first version leaves out.
7. **Open `templates/one-page.md`,** fill it in, and ask the owner to agree it.

Too big for one sitting? Split it into parts that each work alone, and shape only the first.

Done when the owner has said yes to the page. Then hand over: use the skill `write-a-brief` if it needs more detail, or `slice-the-work` if it is ready for tickets.
