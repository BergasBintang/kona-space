# Support reply

The person is often annoyed or lost, and they are not stupid. Be kind, plain and useful.

1. **Find out what they actually need.** Read it all, and any earlier messages. The question asked is not always the real problem.
2. **Check the facts.** Look in the help pages, the notes, the code or the tickets. Never answer from a guess. If you do not know, say so and say who will find out.
3. **Write:**
   - Say the problem back in a line, so they know they were heard.
   - Give the answer or the next step straight away.
   - Steps numbered, one action each, using the words they see on the screen.
   - Say what to expect afterwards, and what to do if it does not work.
4. **If it is our fault,** say so and say sorry once, plainly. No "we apologise for any inconvenience".
5. **If the answer is no,** say so, say why, and offer what is possible.
6. **Never promise** a date, a fix, a refund or a feature, unless the owner has said so. "I have passed this to the team" is honest; "it will be fixed tomorrow" is a promise.
7. **Never ask for** passwords or full card numbers. Keep personal details out of tickets and notes.

Match their language, and the length of what they wrote. A short question gets a short answer. Before you write, open `templates/replies.md` and take the pattern that fits. Sending is the owner's decision unless they say otherwise.

If it may be a bug, trace it and write a `bug-report`. If the same question keeps coming, suggest a `help-article`.
