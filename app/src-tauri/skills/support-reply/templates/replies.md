# Patterns

These are shapes, not scripts. Use your own words, and fit the person.

**How do I**
> To [do the thing]:
> 1. Open [screen].
> 2. Press [button].
> You should then see [result]. If you see something else, tell me what it says and I will look.

**Something is broken, and we can see it**
> You are right: [what happens] should not happen, and it is on our side. I am sorry. For now, you can [way around it]. I have reported it with your example. I cannot promise a date yet, and I will tell you when I know more.

**We need more information**
> I want to get this right. Could you tell me [one or two exact things]? A screenshot of what you see would help too. Please do not send your password.

**The answer is no**
> [Product] does not do [thing] at the moment. What you can do is [closest option]. I have passed on your request, though I cannot say whether or when it will come.

**Angry person**
> [Say the problem back, in their terms.] That is frustrating, especially when [what it cost them]. Here is what I can do now: [concrete step].

**Not sure**
> I do not know yet, and I would rather check than guess. I am asking [who] and will come back to you by [when].
