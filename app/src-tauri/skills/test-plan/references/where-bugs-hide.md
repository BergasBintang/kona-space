# Where bugs hide

**Inputs**
- Empty, spaces only, very long, emoji, non-English letters, quotes and `<script>`.
- Zero, negative, huge, decimals where whole numbers are expected.
- Pasted text with line breaks. A file of the wrong type, huge or empty.

**Order and timing**
- Double click. Back button after saving. Refresh halfway. Two tabs at once.
- Slow network: press something else while waiting.
- Doing the steps in another order than the designer imagined.

**State**
- First use, with nothing there. A very old account. A very full account.
- Logged out while the page is open. No permission halfway through.
- Unsaved work when the window closes.

**Limits**
- The 1st, the last and the one past the last. Page boundaries.
- Dates: end of month, leap day, new year, time zones, summer time.
- Money: rounding, and zero.

**Failure**
- Network drops during saving. The disk is full. The other service is down.
- Does the message say what to do? Is any work lost? Did it half happen?

**After a change**
- What sits next to the change and was not meant to move.
- Old saved files and settings still open.
- Upgrade from the previous version.
