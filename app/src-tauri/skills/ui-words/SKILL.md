# Words on a screen

People read screens in a hurry. Every word should help them do the next thing.

- **Buttons say what happens:** "Delete ticket", not "OK". Keep the same word through the flow: a button "Publish" leads to "Published".
- **Same thing, same word,** everywhere. List the terms the project uses and stick to them.
- **Say it from their side.** "You have no tickets yet", not "Query returned 0 results".
- **Errors** say what happened, whether anything was lost, and what to do. Never blame the person. "Could not save: the connection dropped. Your text is still here. Try again." Never a bare code.
- **Empty states** say what will be here and give the first step.
- **Confirmations** name the thing and what is lost: "Delete Norbit? Its 12 tickets are removed too. This cannot be undone." Not "Are you sure?"
- **Cut.** Remove "please", "successfully", "simply", "in order to". No label that repeats the heading above it.
- **No jokes in errors.** No exclamation marks in a failure.
- **Sentence case,** not Title Case, unless the project does otherwise.
- **Labels stay visible.** A placeholder is an example, not a label.

Give the old and the new side by side with the reason in a few words. Check that the words still fit in the space, and in the languages the project supports.
