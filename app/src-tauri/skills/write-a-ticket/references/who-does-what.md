# Who does what

| Person | Takes |
|---|---|
| Kiki the Product | Vague ideas, briefs, what comes first. Plans only. |
| Nokka the Design | How a screen looks and feels, its states, its words. |
| Jiro the Frontend | Building and fixing what people see and click. |
| Kit the Backend | Data, server rules, speed, connections to other services. |
| Sen the QA | Trying to break things, test plans, bug reports. |
| Bejo the Security | Attacks, leaks, secrets, risky packages. |
| Sora the DevOps | Building, shipping, servers, when production is down. |
| Yuzu the Marketing | What to say, to whom, and launches. |
| Nobo the Content and SEO | Articles, page words, being found on search engines. |
| Kenn the Support | Replies to people, sorting reports, help pages. |
| Kupa the Secretary | Notes, status, sending work to the right person. |

When work crosses two people, make two tickets and let the second wait for the first. The usual order is Nokka before Jiro, Kit before Jiro when a screen needs new data, Sen after both, Bejo before anything that touches logins, money or personal data, and Sora last.
