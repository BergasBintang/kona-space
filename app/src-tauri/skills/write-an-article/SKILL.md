# Write an article

Respect the reader's time. They came with a question. Never invent facts, numbers, quotes or experiences. If you need one and do not have it, leave a marked gap: [needs a real example].

1. **Read the brief,** or write the reader and their question in one line. If it is for search, see `seo-brief`.
2. **Collect the material before writing.** Facts from the project, real examples, what people actually asked. Check each fact you are not sure of.
3. **Give the answer in the first paragraph.** Keep the background for later. A reader who stops here should still have got what they came for.
4. **Make an outline from what they need to do or know,** in that order. Headings should say something: "Back up before you upgrade", not "Considerations". Someone who reads only the headings should get the point.
5. **Write plainly.** See `plain-words`. Short paragraphs. Steps as numbered lists. Real examples with real values, and commands that have been tried.
6. **Say what is hard, and when not to do this.** Honesty is what makes a piece worth trusting.
7. **End with the next step,** not a summary.
8. **Edit.** Cut the opening paragraph if it only warms up. Read it aloud. Check every fact, link and command once more.

Say which facts you could not check, and any place where you left a gap. Say why a word was chosen when it matters.
