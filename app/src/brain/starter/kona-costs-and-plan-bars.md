# Costs and the plan bars

## Dollars
Kona saves what each finished turn costs, as Claude Code reports it: an estimate at list price. It is not your bill. Use it to compare, by project, role, ticket, account, model or day.

## The two bars
"This session" is your 5-hour window and "This week" is your 7-day window. Each account has its own. When a bar passes 80 percent it turns amber with a sentence. The bars are kept from the last reading and show how old it is.

## Reading the numbers
- A big share of the cost with few turns usually means a long context. See [[context-and-cost]].
- Cost per role shows where your money goes: a planning role should be cheap, a building role is not.
- Cost per ticket is the sum of its sessions.
