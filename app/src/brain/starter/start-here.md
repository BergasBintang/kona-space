# Start here

This is Kona's starter brain: short notes on how to work with Claude Code agents and how to use Kona. They ship with Kona, they are read-only, and your own notes can link to them with `[[note names]]`.

## How to ask
Type a question in "Ask the brain". You get one of three answers, in words:
- **Found in the brain:** a section matches well. You see the note and the lines.
- **Partly in the brain:** only loose matches. You see the three closest and a clear note that they are close, not exact.
- **Not in the brain:** nothing matches. Kona offers choices and never acts on its own. The question goes on the wanted list, so the gap shows up later as a to-do.
